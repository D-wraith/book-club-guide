# About the club

## Why we meet

We are building a social club where people trade ideas, connect, and discover possibilities for their lives. Books give us a starting point for talking about purpose, belief, gratitude, direction, and the assumptions underneath our problems. We want people who think they cannot make it to have a place to explore what could help them move forward.

You do not need to buy a book, finish a book, or attend every week to participate. Every meeting stands on its own. Bring your experience, your questions, and a willingness to listen.

## Understanding through our passions

A core goal is to help people recognize their purpose and use their passions or specialties to make ideas understandable to others. A chef might explain a reading concept through a cooking analogy a fifth grader could understand. Any interest or lived experience can contribute; no credential or settled sense of purpose is required.

Ask: “How would you explain this through something you love or know well?” Invite questions and other analogies to build shared understanding. Participation remains optional.

## Rules that keep the club going

- The meeting does not depend on a founder, a particular facilitator, a turnout target, or everyone owning the book.
- If only one person attends, that person can read, take notes, and record the session. It still counts. Anyone is encouraged to keep the practice going independently; there is no homework obligation.
- Any idea or decision that would derail a meeting is automatically vetoed. Apply this reasonably: natural disasters, emergencies, and unsafe conditions can interrupt the plan. Nobody is expected to travel, meet, or complete reading in those circumstances.
- Missing a week creates no catch-up obligation. New and returning people can join the current conversation.

## How we treat each other

Listen without taking over. Speak from experience and ask before giving personal advice. People may pass on any question. Discuss ideas without attacking people. Do not include another person’s sensitive story or identifiable remarks in a shared recap without their permission.
