# Recaps

Short notes from each meeting: the book, the ideas we traded, and what we decided.

## Privacy first

Recaps are never shown publicly until someone has reviewed them for personal information. Keep sensitive personal details out of the recap; leaving them out entirely is best. Only reviewed recaps go on this site; drafts stay private to the organizers. Nobody’s sensitive story or identifiable remarks appear without their permission.

## Recap template

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Meeting date and location | Date, 6–8 p.m. Eastern (proposed), physical location |
| Attendance and roles | Count; note taker, plus opener and usher if someone held them; solo session if applicable |
| Book and topic | Title, edition, chapter or passage, discussion question |
| Ideas exchanged | Main ideas, helpful analogies, connections to passions, disagreements, questions |
| Decisions | What was agreed; proposals still open are marked as such |
| Next meeting | Next Wednesday, 6–8 p.m. (proposed); confirmed venue or pending |

Tasks with owners and due dates are kept in the organizers’ Edit Log, not in public recaps.
