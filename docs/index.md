# Book Club Guide

A weekly ideas club: we read a little, talk a lot, and help each other see new possibilities.

**Version 0.10 · November 23, 2026 · Club name pending**

## Come as you are

You do not need to buy a book, finish a book, or attend every week. Every meeting stands on its own. Bring your experience, your questions, and a willingness to listen.
