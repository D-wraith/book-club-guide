# Books

## First discussion

Our first book is _Psycho-Cybernetics_ by Maxwell Maltz. Our suggested reference is the 2015 updated edition, ISBN 9780399176135. Other editions are welcome—we’ll refer to chapters and topics alongside page numbers so everyone can follow along.

We’ll begin with a question: “What direction would you choose if you did not have to know every step yet?” Together, we’ll explore self-image and what it means to begin before having the whole route figured out.

No prior reading is required.

## Reading without buying

You don’t need to buy a book to participate. We’re working to make print, ebook, audiobook, and accessible options available through lending copies and authorized borrowing or sharing resources.

We’ll share access options as they’re confirmed. If you have a copy you’d like to lend or know of a useful resource, let an organizer know. You’re welcome to join the conversation even if you haven’t obtained a copy.

## Affordable and donated copies

We’re working to build a small lending collection through donated and sponsored books, and we’ll share affordable options as we find them.

Have a copy to lend or donate, or know someone who could help? Let an organizer know. We welcome connections with local book groups, bookstores, and library organizations.

For borrowed copies, we’ll keep a simple lending record and agree on a return date so others can enjoy them too.

## How books are chosen

- Organizers curate a book schedule around connected themes. Each book builds on, deepens, or thoughtfully challenges ideas from the previous one. This gives our conversations continuity while keeping every meeting accessible on its own.
- Some months, participants may be invited to choose between two books selected by the organizers. Both will connect with the themes we’re exploring. We’ll announce the selection and where it fits in the schedule.
- When questions or ideas develop across several meetings, we may adjust the order of upcoming books to explore them further. Any changes will be intentional and announced ahead of time.

**Under consideration:** Alternating between two connected books on Wednesdays, with each week’s title announced in advance. This approach is still being explored. As always, no prior reading is required.
