First book (confirmed): *Psycho-Cybernetics* by Maxwell Maltz. The proposed reference edition is the 2015 updated edition, ISBN 9780399176135. Other editions are welcome; identify passages by chapter or topic as well as page number.

## How books are chosen

- Organizers set a concrete schedule of books with a consistent thematic direction. Each book should build on, deepen, or thoughtfully challenge ideas from the previous one. The schedule gives the club continuity while each meeting remains accessible on its own.
- As an optional way to involve participants, offer two organizer-selected books for the upcoming month. Both must fit the planned direction; the choice is limited to those two rather than an open-ended selection process. Once chosen, publish the book and its place in the schedule.
- A sustained line of questions or ideas across meetings can justify moving another scheduled book forward. Organizers should review the recaps, record the reason, and announce the revised order. Do not change books on a whim or because of the mood of a single meetup. Thoughtful development of ideas, rather than fluctuating group energy, guides a change.

**Under consideration:** Courtney proposed alternating two connected books on Wednesdays, with a clear thematic link and each week’s title announced beforehand. No prior reading required. This is not an adopted schedule change.
